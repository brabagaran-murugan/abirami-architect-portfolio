About

Architecture rooted in earth tones, quiet details, and thoughtful technology.

I’m Abirami Mohan, an architect focused on crafting spaces that feel grounded, warm, and intuitive. My work blends natural materials, soft palettes, and emerging tools like AI-assisted visualization to explore new possibilities in spatial design.

From residential retreats to community spaces, I’m interested in how architecture can slow us down, invite reflection, and respond gently to its surroundings.

  • Earthy minimalism
  • Human-centered design
  • AI-assisted concept studies
  • Sustainable thinking

Focus areas

Residential, small-scale public spaces, interior architecture, and conceptual studies.

Tools

Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, Adobe Suite, AI visualization tools, hand sketching.

Design process

A calm, iterative journey from context to crafted detail.

01

Listen & observe

Understanding site, climate, and the everyday rituals of the people who will inhabit the space.

02

Explore & sketch

Hand sketches, physical models, and AI-assisted visual studies to test light, massing, and mood.

03

Refine & detail

Translating concepts into buildable drawings, material palettes, and tactile experiences.

04

Collaborate & deliver

Working closely with clients, consultants, and makers to bring the design to life.
